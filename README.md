# VU Study Tracker

## What it is

A free, offline study tracker for VU students preparing for midterm and final-term exams. It keeps track of which lectures you’ve prepared and tells you what to study each day.

## How it helps

- Shows exactly how much of your semester, midterm and final-term syllabus is done.
- Gives you a clear list of lectures for today, so you don’t have to decide what to study.
- Spreads the remaining lectures across the days left, so you don’t cram at the end.
- Keeps the last two days before each exam free for revision.
- Catches up automatically if you miss a day by recalculating from what’s left.
- Keeps your own notes in one place for quick revision.

## Features

- Guided setup: your name, subjects, lecture numbers, exam dates.
- Any number of subjects, and subjects can be added later.
- Works with VU’s exact lecture numbers (like `1, 3, 4, 7-10, 19 & 20`), not just 1 to 45.
- Separate midterm and final-term syllabus for each subject.
- Home screen with exam countdowns and separate progress for the whole semester, midterm and final-term.
- A square for every lecture, so you can see what’s prepared and what’s planned for today.
- Today screen with a tick-off checklist and a switch between midterm and final-term.
- Add or remove lectures from today’s list yourself.
- Notes on any lecture, plus a Notes screen to search, edit and delete them.
- Edit subject names, lecture numbers and exam dates anytime, with a warning before anything is deleted.
- Saves automatically in your browser, with no account or internet needed.
- Works on phone, tablet and desktop in a dark theme.
- Reset Semester to start fresh each term.

## Good to know

- Data is stored only in your browser, so clearing site data erases it.
- There is no cloud sync, so progress doesn’t carry between devices.
